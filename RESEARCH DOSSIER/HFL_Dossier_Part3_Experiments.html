E9SADP — Adaptive Per-Group Privacy Budget

Tests: whether splitting the budget across groups beats a uniform allocation at equal total ε.

Conclusion — NEGATIVE It runs correctly and underperforms uniform DP by construction. The cross-group composition tax drives σ from 11.3 to 70. Retained as a characterised negative result, not a claimed contribution. The transferable lesson: splitting a budget across groups is paid for at a punitive rate under composition.

E10Gradient Compression and Uplink Measurement

Tests: actual bytes on the wire under top-k sparsification plus 8-bit quantisation with error feedback.

top-20 % sparsity × 8-bit → 20× nominal · error-feedback accumulator · uplink_measured_*.json
MeasurementBytes / round
Full-model FP32 FedAvg258,931,920
Head-only, uncompressed67,277,400
Observed, post-fix16,821,300

Defect found and fixed: the compressor took a fixed k = numel × sparsity of every tensor, so frozen encoders — whose delta is exactly zero — contributed 20 % of their elements as explicit zeros. Omitting all-zero tensors accounted for a 78.87 % reduction on its own.

Conclusion Uplink reduction 90.82 % (VitalDB) and 96.08 % (WEAR) against a 50 % target. The fix was proven not to alter learning: the dense path is bit-identical to the previous implementation over 5 rounds, and an isolated re-run moved AUC by 0.0001.

E12INT8 Quantisation for Edge Deployment

Tests: the prescribed INT8 export, expected to be faster and smaller.

Conclusion — NEGATIVE INT8 is 1.90× slower on CPU and 3.25× slower on GPU than FP32, and misses the 4 MB size target at 6.55 MB. Two independent causes were identified: dynamic quantisation only accelerates MatMul/Gemm, while this model's cost is dominated by convolutions and a recurrent path; and on CUDA the quantised ops have no GPU kernel and fall back to CPU — the runtime reports 3180 Memcpy nodes inserted, which is exactly the 3.25× penalty. Deploy the FP32 artifact. If size later matters more than latency, the correct lever is static/QDQ quantisation or quantisation-aware training.

E18k-Scaling Law — the Headline Result

Tests: whether the cost of a patient-level guarantee is a fixed penalty or a function of how many records each patient contributes.

All 24 configurations: k ∈ {1,2,4,8} × 3 seeds × 2 arms · per-step budget and units-per-step held constant · k_scaling/vitaldb/k_scaling_vitaldb.csv
kSample arm AUPRCPatient arm AUPRCGap95 % CIp
10.50150.5067−0.0052[−0.0664, +0.0561]0.75
20.55900.5084+0.0507[+0.0417, +0.0596]0.0017
40.59240.5092+0.0832[+0.0694, +0.0971]0.0015
80.59550.5089+0.0866[+0.0603, +0.1129]0.0049
Conclusion — THE CORE FINDING The cost is a curve in k, not a number: zero at k=1, growing monotonically, saturating by k=8. The experiment carries its own positive control: at k=1 a patient is a record, so the two guarantees are identical and the arms must collapse — they do (p=0.75). The mechanism is asymmetric and must be stated that way: the patient arm is flat in k (0.4967–0.5176 throughout) while the sample arm rises (0.5015→0.5955). The widening gap is the sample arm improving, not the patient arm degrading — extra records per patient buy the patient arm nothing, because noise scales with the patient's total contribution.

Honest limits: AUROC shows the same monotone shape (+0.0006 → +0.0094 → +0.0133 → +0.0132) but no k reaches significance. Report AUPRC as the resolving metric and AUROC as directionally consistent but underpowered at n=3. Accuracy is pinned at 0.8005 in every patient row — an uncalibrated 0.5 threshold on imbalanced data, do not quote it.

4  —  The Revival Gate

E24LiRA at ε=16 — N-A's Last Chance

Tests: whether N-A failed because ε=1 crushed both arms to chance, leaving no room for a gap to appear. At ε=16 the noise is 13× lower (σ 11.33 → 0.85), so a membership signal should survive if one exists.

Attack variantPaired Δ (record − patient)95 % CISignificant?Reading

Conclusion — N-A CLOSED PERMANENTLY The four attacks contradict each other. Two report a significant gap in the opposite direction to the prediction, one reports a significant gap an eighth of the predicted size, and the principled two-sided variant reports chance. Mutually inconsistent estimators are the signature of no underlying signal, with each estimator being driven by a different facet of the difficulty confound.

Decisive on its own terms: the record-level arm sits 0.0278 BELOW its own difficulty floor. It carries no membership signal at all, so there is nothing for a record-versus-patient gap to be made of. Loosening the budget sixteenfold changed the record arm's attack AUC by 0.0012 (0.5477 → 0.5465).

The “no room at ε=1” hypothesis is refuted. N-A did not fail for want of headroom; it failed because the effect is not there. This closes the question rather than leaving it open at n=5, and it does so with the difficulty floor as a witness. Do not re-run at higher budgets — the ceiling has been tested and the answer did not change.
