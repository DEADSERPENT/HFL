  PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT

  Five extensions beyond the core HFL-MM system were proposed. This
  file records, for each one, three things kept strictly separate:

    PROPOSED    what the idea is
    BUILT       whether it exists in the training path
    MEASURED    what happened when it was tested

  An idea that is proposed but not built is not a contribution. An
  idea that is built but measured to hurt is a negative result, and is
  reported as one. Neither may be presented as a strength.

  This separation exists because the earlier framing of this work
  advertised five contributions without distinguishing between these
  states. Correcting that is the point of this revision.

  Of five proposed extensions, ZERO are currently defensible as
  positive contributions. One is unbuilt, one is negative, one is
  null, one is excluded, and one is untested.

  All measurements above were obtained on a previous data pipeline and

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  SECTION 2: I1 — CROSS-MODAL GATED ATTENTION FUSION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  PROPOSED
    Replace late concatenation with cross-attention between the two
    modality streams, so each encoder can attend to the other's
    representation and the fusion can down-weight a modality that is
    missing or unreliable at inference time.

  BUILT: NO.
    A design file exists. It has zero references from the training
    path. No run has ever executed it.

  WHY IT WAS NOT ADOPTED — and this is a real architectural tension,
  not merely a matter of time:

    The privacy design of this system depends on the fusion head being
    a SMALL, SEPARABLE block sitting on top of frozen encoders. That
    separability is what makes head-only DP fine-tuning possible: there
    is a compact, low-dimensional component to privately train while
    the encoders stay fixed.

    Cross-attention entangles the two streams throughout the network.
    There is then no small separable head left to DP-train, and the
    head-only recipe — which is the strongest part of this work —
    cannot be applied. The two ideas are in direct conflict.

  HONEST POSITION
    I1 is not a contribution of this thesis. It is a documented design
    alternative that was rejected for a stated architectural reason.
    Presenting it as an implemented innovation would be false.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  SECTION 3: I2 — ADAPTIVE PER-LAYER PRIVACY ALLOCATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  PROPOSED
    Instead of one clipping norm and one noise multiplier for the whole
    model, group parameters by depth and allocate a different clip and
    noise scale to each group, on the reasoning that layers differ in
    gradient scale and in sensitivity.

  BUILT: YES — implemented and selectable at runtime.

  MEASURED: WORSE than a uniform budget.

  WHY IT LOSES
    Splitting one mechanism into several does not split the privacy
    cost proportionally. Each group is its own release, and the
    composition across groups charges more total budget than a single
    release of the whole model does. To stay within the same overall
    epsilon, every group must therefore be noisier than the single
    uniform mechanism would have been. The adaptivity buys a better
    ALLOCATION of noise and pays for it with MORE noise overall, and
    on this system the second effect dominates.

  VALUE OF THIS RESULT
    This is a useful negative finding and is worth reporting. The
    literature proposes adaptive privacy allocation frequently and
    ablates it against a uniform budget rarely. A clean measurement
    showing when it does not pay, with the composition-tax explanation,
    is a legitimate contribution — as a negative result, stated as one.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  SECTION 4: I3 — STALENESS-WEIGHTED AGGREGATION (ASWA)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  PROPOSED
    Weight each client's contribution at the edge by how stale its
    update is, so that a slow client's out-of-date update does not
    drag the edge model backwards.

  BUILT: YES — wired into the edge aggregation and running in every
    hierarchical run.

  MEASURED: NOTHING. It has never been ablated.

  WHAT THIS MEANS
    ASWA is ON in every reported hierarchical result, so its effect is
    baked into those numbers and cannot be separated from them. It
    cannot be claimed as a contribution, because no comparison against
    plain FedAvg weighting at the edge has been run.

  WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT
    An arm identical to the full system with staleness weighting
    replaced by uniform sample-count weighting, paired by seed. Until
    that exists, ASWA is an implementation detail of the system, not a
    result of it.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  SECTION 5: I4 — EDGE-SIDE KNOWLEDGE DISTILLATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  PROPOSED
    After edge aggregation, distil the aggregated edge model against
    its client models so the edge model absorbs client-specific
    knowledge that plain averaging discards.

  BUILT: YES.

  EXCLUDED FROM EVERY REPORTED RUN, and the reason is specific:

    The distillation step is NOT freeze-aware. Under the head-only
    recipe the encoders are frozen and only the fusion head is
    trained and transmitted. The distillation implementation does not
    know this, and would distil across parameters that are not being
    trained — producing updates to weights that are supposed to be
    fixed, silently invalidating both the freeze and the privacy
    accounting that assumes it.

    Rather than report results from a configuration whose privacy
    guarantee does not describe what ran, the stage is disabled.

  TO MAKE IT USABLE
    Restrict distillation to trainable parameters, then ablate it as a
    paired arm. Until then it contributes nothing and is not claimed.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  SECTION 6: I5 — PERSONALISED LOCAL ADAPTATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  PROPOSED
    Give each client a small local adapter fine-tuned on its own data,
    so a client whose distribution differs from the federation average
    is not forced onto a single global model. On a genuinely non-IID
    federation this should help the extreme clients most.

  BUILT: YES.

  MEASURED: no meaningful effect on the configuration tested.

  A CAVEAT WORTH KEEPING
    Personalisation has the strongest case exactly where client
    distributions differ most. The current primary dataset has clients
    whose positive-class base rate ranges from 3.2 % to 50.7 %, which
    is a substantially more heterogeneous federation than the setting
    in which the null result was obtained.

    So the null result should not be treated as settled for this study.
    It should be re-measured on the current data, per client rather
    than pooled — a method that helps the two extreme clients and does
    nothing for the middle seven will show no pooled effect at all
    while still being useful. Reporting only the pooled number would
    hide exactly the effect the method is designed to produce.

  SECTION 7: WHERE THE ACTUAL CONTRIBUTION LIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  With all five extensions in the states above, the defensible
  contributions of this work are not among them. They are:

  C1  HEAD-ONLY DP FINE-TUNING as a recipe for hierarchical multimodal
      FL under a strict budget, together with the isolating experiment
      that shows what it is worth (B4 → B6 for the recipe, B6 → the
      proposed system for the topology, S3 → S4 for privacy cost at
      fixed topology). The mechanism is not merely "freeze and train
      less"; the claim is that the same sigma becomes survivable
      because the optimisation geometry changes, and the design is
      constructed so that this is testable rather than asserted.

  C2  A GENUINELY PAIRED MULTIMODAL FL BENCHMARK, with the fusion gain
      demonstrated against BOTH unimodal arms rather than the weaker
      one, on data whose two modalities were captured by one
      instrument rather than joined by a rule.

  C3  A NEGATIVE RESULT on adaptive privacy allocation, with the
      composition-tax explanation for why it loses.

  C4  METHODOLOGICAL RIGOUR AS AN ARTIFACT: leakage-controlled splits
      appropriate to each data type, per-dataset scoring units, an
      objective checker that reports NOT MEASURED rather than guessing,
      and a traceability matrix binding every documented deliverable to
      the code or artifact that satisfies it.

  C1 and C2 are the paper. C3 is a section. C4 is what makes the rest
  believable.
