\begin{abstract}
Multimodal Internet-of-Things deployments in clinical settings generate streams
that cannot be centralised; federated learning removes that obstacle and a
hierarchical organization relieves the wide-area link, but neither supplies a
privacy guarantee. The guarantee such systems report is stated over training
records while the one a clinical setting requires concerns individuals, and
wherever an episode of care yields many records the two differ for every
participant: converting between them after the fact degrades a budget of
$\varepsilon \approx 1$ to $\varepsilon \approx 8$ at an unusable $\delta$.
This paper presents a three-tier framework in which the privacy unit is the
patient by construction --- gradients are grouped by individual before they are
bounded, the noise scale is calibrated against every client so that the edge
tier enters the reported budget, and the conformal calibration quantile is
charged to that budget rather than omitted from it. Accounted directly the
guarantee costs $1.77\times$ the noise of a record-level one, not eight times
the budget. Encoders pretrained on a population disjoint from every client are
frozen, so only a compact fusion head is privatised and transmitted: the
composed budget is $\varepsilon \le 1$, uplink traffic falls by $90.8\,\%$ and
$96.1\,\%$, and $95$th-percentile inference latency is $3.0$~ms. Three findings
qualify the design rather than advertise it. Pretrained initialisation, not the
restriction of noise to the head, is what makes a strict budget survivable: as
the budget tightens from $\varepsilon = \infty$ to $\varepsilon = 0.5$, a
full-model private arm trained from a random start loses $0.118$ in area under
the ROC curve while the same arm from a pretrained start loses nothing
measurable, and restricting noise to the head adds no accuracy over that at any
budget. The cost
of the guarantee grows with the records each individual contributes and
vanishes where the units coincide, so the experiment controls itself; against a
forecast frozen before an external corpus of $187$ hospitals was built it
reproduces in shape but not in magnitude. Fusion is likewise conditional, worth
$0.0370$ in area under the ROC curve where the task leaves headroom and nothing
where one modality already suffices. An individual-level membership-inference
audit, reported alongside a positive control on a configuration known to
memorise, refutes the leakage difference the design was predicted to produce
without establishing that the two guarantees are equivalent.
\end{abstract}
