\section*{Data Availability}
VitalDB \cite{ref20} is openly available from its authors
(\url{https://vitaldb.net}) under the CC BY 4.0 license; this study retrieved
twelve monitor tracks and the clinical and laboratory tables through the
authors' open API (\url{https://api.vitaldb.net}). WEAR
\cite{ref21} is publicly available from its authors
(\url{https://github.com/mariusbock/wear}; download via
\url{https://bit.ly/wear_dataset}) under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license; this
study uses its released 50~Hz inertial data and precomputed I3D video features.
The eICU Collaborative Research Database \cite{ref27}, version~2.0
(\url{https://doi.org/10.13026/C2WM1R}), is available through PhysioNet
\cite{ref28} to credentialed users who complete the required training and sign
the PhysioNet Credentialed Health Data Use Agreement~1.5.0; this study uses its
patient, hospital, APACHE result and periodic vital-sign tables. Accordingly, no patient-level records or arrays derived from them are
redistributed. All corpora are de-identified; this study is a secondary
analysis with no patient contact. Code and aggregate results will be released
upon publication.
