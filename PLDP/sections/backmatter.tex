\section*{Data Availability}
VitalDB \cite{ref20} (\url{https://vitaldb.net}) is publicly available. WEAR
\cite{ref21} is publicly available from its authors
(\url{https://github.com/mariusbock/wear}; download via
\url{https://bit.ly/wear_dataset}) under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license; this
study uses its released 50~Hz inertial data and precomputed I3D video features.
The eICU Collaborative Research Database \cite{ref27} is
available through PhysioNet \cite{ref28} to credentialed users under a data
use agreement, so no patient-level records or arrays derived from them are
redistributed. All corpora are de-identified; this study is a secondary
analysis with no patient contact. Code and aggregate results will be released
upon publication.
