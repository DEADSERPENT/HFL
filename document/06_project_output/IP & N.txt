  A claim enters this register ONLY if three conditions hold:

    1. IMPLEMENTED   it exists in the training path and executes
    2. MEASURABLE    an experiment exists that could refute it
    3. BOUNDED       its scope is limited to what that experiment
                     actually covers

  Every entry therefore carries a PROVING EXPERIMENT — the specific
  run that will establish or refute it — named in advance, together
  with the artifact that will record the outcome and the result that
  would REFUTE the claim.

  Naming the refutation up front is deliberate. A claim whose failure
  condition is only decided after seeing the data is not a claim, it
  is a description written to fit whatever happened.

  Each entry is marked:

    TO PROVE       experiment defined and built; run pending
    PROVEN         experiment run; claim survived
    NEGATIVE       experiment run; claim did not hold, reported as such

  A claim with no experiment that could refute it does not enter this
  register at all.

  WHY VERSION 2.0 IS MUCH SHORTER THAN VERSION 1.0

  The previous register listed seventeen claims. Most described systems
  that were never built, and several rested on a multimodal pairing
  that turned out to be manufactured by a rule rather than recorded by
  an instrument — which meant the "fusion" they claimed to exploit was
  an artefact of the pairing, not a property of the world.

  A register that lists unbuilt systems is worse than no register: it
  invites a thesis chapter to be written around a contribution that
  cannot be defended in a viva. This version lists only what can be
  shown. Ideas that were dropped are recorded in Section 6 as design
  history, clearly separated, so the thinking is preserved without the
  claims being overstated.

  SECTION 2: IP-1 — HEAD-ONLY DP FINE-TUNING FOR HIERARCHICAL
             MULTIMODAL FEDERATED LEARNING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  THE CLAIM
    A method for training a multimodal federated model under a strict
    privacy budget in which: (a) both modality encoders are pretrained
    on data disjoint from every client's private data and then frozen;
    (b) differential-privacy stochastic gradient descent is applied ONLY
    to the late-fusion head; and (c) only the head delta is aggregated
    hierarchically and transmitted — such that a noise multiplier large
    enough to satisfy a strict budget, which prevents convergence when
    applied to the full network, is tolerated by the head.

  THE PRECISE MECHANISM — and the point most likely to be misread

    Freezing does NOT reduce the noise. Sigma is determined by the
    budget, the number of steps and the sampling rate. It has no
    dependence on the number of trained parameters. Freezing changes
    the OPTIMISATION GEOMETRY: a small head over frozen, already-
    discriminative features is close to a linear probe — low-
    dimensional and well-conditioned — which DP-SGD optimises robustly
    at a sigma that destroys end-to-end training from scratch.

    Stating this correctly matters for the claim's validity. "We froze
    most of the model so we needed less noise" would be false and would
    not survive review.

  WHY IT IS NOVEL
    Parameter-efficient private fine-tuning is established in
    centralised settings. What is not established is its use as the
    mechanism that makes a strict budget viable in a HIERARCHICAL,
    MULTIMODAL federation — where the frozen/trained boundary
    simultaneously determines what is transmitted on the uplink, and
    therefore couples the privacy result to the communication result.
    The same design decision answers Objective 1 and Objective 2.

  SCOPE LIMIT THAT MUST TRAVEL WITH THIS CLAIM
    The claim is valid only when the encoders are pretrained on data
    DISJOINT from client data. If they are pretrained on pooled client
    training data, the encoders have seen private data non-privately
    and the reported epsilon is an upper bound on achievable utility,
    not a guarantee. The implementation enforces the disjointness check
    and refuses overlapping indices; a genuinely external public corpus
    is still required and is the single largest outstanding item.

  EVIDENCE
    model/training/hfl_trainer.py (--freeze_encoders)
    model/training/pretrain_encoders.py (--public_data)
    model/training/epsilon_sweep.py  → epsilon_sweep.csv
  PROVING EXPERIMENT
    The epsilon sweep runs BOTH recipes — full-model DP and head-only
    DP — at every budget, on shared seeds, and plots the gap between
    them as epsilon tightens. The claim predicts that gap WIDENS as the
    budget gets stricter: at a loose budget both recipes work, and only
    under real noise pressure should freezing matter.

  WHAT WOULD REFUTE IT
    The two curves failing to separate as epsilon tightens, or the
    head-only curve separating equally at loose and strict budgets —
    the latter would mean the gain comes from training fewer parameters
    generally, not from surviving noise, which is a different claim.

  STATUS: TO PROVE — built and wired; run pending.

  SECTION 3: IP-2 — TWO-AXIS DECOMPOSITION OF A PRIVACY RESULT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  THE CLAIM
    An evaluation design that separates the two changes a hierarchical
    private FL system makes relative to flat private FL — the TOPOLOGY
    (adding an edge tier) and the RECIPE (restricting DP to a frozen-
    encoder head) — by constructing the two intermediate arms that hold
    one axis fixed while moving the other:

      flat + full-model DP  →  flat + head-only DP     = recipe effect
      flat + head-only DP   →  hierarchical + head-only = topology effect
      hierarchical, no DP   →  hierarchical + DP        = privacy cost

    with all arms sharing data, partitions, seed, budget and sigma.

  WHY THIS IS A CONTRIBUTION AND NOT MERE BOOKKEEPING
    A proposed hierarchical private system almost always differs from
    its nearest published baseline in more than one way at once.
    Reporting that it wins therefore establishes that the COMBINATION
    wins, and says nothing about which part earned it. Papers routinely
    attribute the gain to whichever component they are selling.

    The intermediate arms are what convert an aggregate win into an
    attributable one. They are cheap to construct and rarely present.

  A NECESSARY IMPLEMENTATION DETAIL
    The no-privacy arm must be genuinely no-privacy: no clipping, no
    noise, no accountant. A large epsilon is NOT equivalent, because
    per-sample gradient clipping still applies and changes the
    optimisation. Using a loose budget as a stand-in for "no privacy"
    understates what privacy costs, in the flattering direction.

  EVIDENCE
    model/training/baselines.py       (B4 vs B6)
    scripts/joint_eval.py             (S2 → S3 → S4)
    model/training/hfl_trainer.py     (--no_dp)
  PROVING EXPERIMENT
    Run all four arms at a shared budget and seed, then check that the
    recipe effect and the topology effect ACCOUNT FOR the end-to-end
    difference between flat full-model DP and the proposed system.

  WHAT WOULD REFUTE IT
    The two intermediate contrasts summing to materially less than the
    end-to-end difference — which would mean the two axes interact and
    cannot be attributed separately, and the decomposition claim fails
    even if the system still wins overall.

  STATUS: TO PROVE — all four arms built; run pending.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  SECTION 4: IP-3 — EXPONENTIAL-MECHANISM DP QUANTILE FOR FEDERATED
             CONFORMAL PREDICTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  THE CLAIM
    A federated conformal prediction procedure in which each client's
    local calibration quantile is released under differential privacy
    using the EXPONENTIAL MECHANISM over the empirical score
    distribution, rather than by additive noise, and the private
    quantiles are aggregated hierarchically — with the calibration
    budget composed into the system's total reported epsilon.

  WHY THE MECHANISM CHOICE IS THE CLAIM
    A quantile is a value drawn from the data, not an average over it.
    Additive noise calibrated to a strict budget is comparable in
    magnitude to the spread of the statistic itself, so the released
    quantile is dominated by noise: the resulting prediction sets are
    either vacuous (every label included) or invalid (coverage lost).

    The exponential mechanism SELECTS a value from the observed score
    distribution with probability weighted by utility. Its output is
    therefore always a plausible quantile of real data. Measured on
    calibration data, its bias was ~0.001 against an additive
    mechanism's standard deviation of ~0.416 on the same statistic —
    about three orders of magnitude better behaved.

  THE PART MOST SYSTEMS GET WRONG
    Calibration consumes privacy budget. A system that reports only its
    training epsilon and performs a data-dependent calibration step has
    understated its true budget. Here calibration runs on the final
    round only and its epsilon is composed into the reported total.

  WHY IT MATTERS CLINICALLY
    A point prediction of an outcome is not actionable at the bedside.
    A calibrated set with a stated coverage level is. Delivering that
    under a formal privacy budget, in a federation, is the useful
    combination.

  EVIDENCE
    model/utils/fedconform_hc.py
  PROVING EXPERIMENT
    Calibrate on the held-out calibration split, then measure EMPIRICAL
    COVERAGE on the test split: the fraction of test cases whose true
    label falls inside the predicted set. Compare against the nominal
    level, and compare set sizes against an additive-noise mechanism at
    the same budget.

  WHAT WOULD REFUTE IT
    Empirical coverage falling materially below nominal (the sets are
    invalid), or set sizes so large that every label is included (the
    sets are vacuous). Either outcome means the private quantile is not
    usable, regardless of how well-behaved the statistic looks.

  STATUS: TO PROVE — mechanism implemented; coverage run pending.

  SECTION 5: NOVELTY CONTRIBUTIONS (N-SERIES)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  N-1  A GENUINELY-PAIRED MULTIMODAL CLINICAL FL BENCHMARK, WITH A
       BOTH-ARMS FUSION TEST
       STATUS: PROVEN

    Contribution has two parts, and the second is the harder one.

    (a) The benchmark. Two modalities recorded by ONE acquisition
        system from ONE patient during ONE operation: intraoperative
        vital-sign trends and the preoperative clinical vector for the
        same case. The pairing is a property of the instrument. There
        is no pairing rule, so there is no pairing artefact.

    (b) The test protocol. Fusion must beat BOTH unimodal arms by more
        than the seed standard deviation, with each arm RETRAINED on
        its own inputs rather than fed zeros. Beating only the weaker
        arm is not evidence — a fused model reaches that by ignoring
        the weak modality. Feeding zeros measures what happens when a
        modality breaks, not what it is worth.

    MEASURED (VitalDB, centralised, non-private, 2 seeds):
        signals only   AUROC 0.8283   AUPRC 0.5281
        clinical only  AUROC 0.7700   AUPRC 0.5022
        FUSED          AUROC 0.8694   AUPRC 0.6343
        gain over best unimodal: +0.0411 AUROC, +0.1062 AUPRC
        = 14.1x the across-arm seed standard deviation

    BOUNDED: this holds on VitalDB. The wearable dataset is at ceiling
    (best single modality 0.9976 AUROC) and CANNOT evidence fusion; its
    +0.0004 gain is reported as a ceiling effect and is not counted as
    corroboration. The protocol writes this verdict into its own output
    so a downstream reader cannot mistake one for the other.

    EVIDENCE: scripts/modality_ablation.py → ablation_vitaldb.json


  N-2  MISSINGNESS-AS-SIGNAL ENCODING FOR INTRAOPERATIVE FL
       STATUS: TO PROVE

    Operating-theatre monitoring is intermittently observed: cuff
    pressures arrive every few minutes, probes detach, lines are
    flushed. The standard treatment is imputation, which discards the
    fact that a gap existed.

    WHICH signals are being watched is itself clinically informative —
    an anaesthetist escalates monitoring when a patient deteriorates,
    so the observation pattern correlates with the outcome being
    predicted. Each of the 9 signal channels therefore carries a
    parallel binary observed-mask channel, giving the 18-channel input.

    Contribution: applying missingness-as-signal in a FEDERATED
    setting, where the missingness pattern is itself client-specific —
    different surgical specialties monitor differently — so the mask
    channels carry part of the non-IID structure the federation must
    handle.

    PROVING EXPERIMENT: train the identical model on the 9 signal
    channels ALONE, dropping the 9 mask channels, and compare paired by
    seed. The claim predicts a measurable loss.

    WHAT WOULD REFUTE IT: no loss, meaning the observation pattern
    carries nothing the signals do not already say — in which case the
    18-channel design is unnecessary complexity and should be dropped.

    This ablation is NOT yet wired. It must be added before N-2 may be
    stated as a contribution.


  N-3  SPECIALTY-DERIVED NON-IID PARTITION
       STATUS: PROVEN — measured property of the data

    Federated learning papers overwhelmingly synthesise non-IID
    conditions with a Dirichlet parameter chosen to make the problem
    look hard. Here the clients are surgical operation types, and the
    heterogeneity is a measured property of clinical practice: the
    positive-class base rate spans 3.2 % to 50.7 % across the nine
    clients, a sixteen-fold spread.

    Contribution: a federation whose non-IID structure is real,
    reproducible from the source data, and explainable to a clinician —
    rather than a knob setting. Includes the practical handling that
    such data forces: operation types with too few positive cases are
    merged so that no client holds a single class, since a single-class
    client has undefined AUROC and is otherwise silently dropped from
    any average over clients.

    EVIDENCE: data/loaders/preprocess_vitaldb.py, partition_meta.json,
    and the measured heatmap (Fig. 8).


  N-4  THE COMPOSITION TAX OF ADAPTIVE PER-LAYER PRIVACY ALLOCATION
       STATUS: NEGATIVE — and reported as a result

    Adaptive privacy allocation is frequently proposed and rarely
    ablated against a uniform budget. Implemented here and measured
    WORSE than uniform.

    The explanation is the contribution: splitting one mechanism into
    per-group mechanisms does not split the privacy cost
    proportionally. Each group is a separate release and composition
    across groups charges more total budget than one release of the
    whole model. Holding overall epsilon fixed therefore forces every
    group to be noisier than the single uniform mechanism would have
    been. Adaptivity buys better ALLOCATION and pays in MORE noise; on
    this system the second effect dominates.

    A clean negative result with a mechanism-level explanation is
    publishable and useful. It is reported as negative, not omitted.

    Must be re-measured on the current datasets before being quoted.


  N-5  CASE-LEVEL SCORING FOR WINDOW-BASED CLINICAL FL
       STATUS: PROVEN — method in force across every result

    The model consumes 10-minute windows; the clinical question is
    about the operation. Windows within one case are strongly
    correlated, so window-level metrics overstate the effective sample
    size and flatter the model.

    Contribution: every result is reported at BOTH levels — window
    (what is optimised) and case (each case's window probabilities
    averaged into one prediction per operation). The split is by case
    so no operation spans train and test, and the aggregation asserts
    that no case carries conflicting labels.

    This is the statistically honest and clinically meaningful unit,
    and it is what should be quoted. Reporting only window-level
    metrics on this kind of data is a common and material overstatement.

    EVIDENCE: _compute_metrics() group aggregation; case_auc /
    case_auprc columns in every result file.

  SECTION 6: DESIGN HISTORY — IDEAS CONSIDERED AND NOT CLAIMED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Recorded to preserve the reasoning. NONE of these is a contribution
  of this thesis and none may be cited as one.

  Cross-modal gated attention fusion
    Not built. Rejected for a stated architectural reason: it entangles
    the modality streams throughout the network, leaving no small
    separable head to DP-train — which would forfeit IP-1, the
    strongest claim in this register. The two designs conflict.

  Edge-side knowledge distillation
    Built but not freeze-aware: it would update parameters that are
    supposed to be frozen, invalidating both the freeze and the privacy
    accounting that assumes it. Disabled in every reported run.

  Staleness-weighted aggregation
    Built and running, never ablated. Its effect is baked into every
    hierarchical result and cannot be separated from them, so it is an
    implementation detail rather than a claim.

  Personalised local adaptation
    Built; no measurable pooled effect. Worth one re-measurement on the
    current data reported PER CLIENT — a method that helps only the two
    extreme clients and not the middle seven shows nothing in a pooled
    average while still being useful. The pooled null is not conclusive.

  Foundation-model adaptation, generative gradient-free training,
  cohort-shuffling amplification, contrastive patient similarity,
  real-time deterioration alerting, continual learning across new
  diagnostic classes
    Proposed in the earlier planning of this project. None was built.
    They are recorded as future directions, not as contributions.
